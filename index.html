Film class · why LED wins

Emissive strip. Projector film is a demo, then delete.

LED-film makes its own light. ClearBright™ is a nanoparticle projector scatter film (MIT-origin, Lux Labs) — not an LED film. It only shows an image when a projector hits it. Their copy: sunlight makes the film invisible.

  • Scatter film. Image only with a projector on it.
  • Not an LED film. Do not call it one.
  • Sunlight makes the film invisible — their copy.
  • Dealers want 1000–3500 lumens for indoor rooms.
